About Staurus Foods

Established in Nigeria. Building an African sourcing platform.

Staurus Foods Limited was incorporated in Nigeria in 2020. Our initial sourcing capability combines buyer development with a Nigeria-based business partnership. Our long-term vision is to develop verified sourcing networks across relevant African markets.

Our identity

Reliable relationships, documented specifications.

We are developing an African-focused export sourcing operation for shelf-stable agricultural produce, starting in Nigeria. Our starting model is intentionally disciplined: first verify buyer requirements, agree samples and payment terms, and then source goods for shipment.

Our goal is long-term commercial partnerships with processors, ingredient importers and commodity distributors, beginning with modest orders and scaling only when quality and fulfilment are proven.

Company registration documentation has been supplied, but current status, certification and export registrations are being independently reconfirmed.

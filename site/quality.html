Quality and trade

Specifications before shipments.

Each transaction begins with the buyer's requirements, not an assumed universal grade.

Our proposed controls.

  • Identify supplier, origin, harvest year and traceable lot.
  • Request representative samples and relevant testing.
  • Confirm buyer-defined moisture, purity, defect and safety standards.
  • Use independent inspection when required by the contract.
  • Agree export documentation, Incoterms and payment terms before procurement.
  • Protect dry goods against contamination, pests and condensation in transit.

Certifications such as organic, non-GMO, phytosanitary or EU deforestation traceability are not claimed unless evidenced for the specific product and supply chain.

Priority sourcing · Nigerian origin

Dried hibiscus flowers for international ingredient buyers

Staurus Foods is developing buyer-led sourcing of dried Nigerian hibiscus, also known as zobo or roselle, for bulk tea, beverage and botanical ingredient applications. Start with a product specification and sample enquiry.

One of our initial sourcing priorities

Specification-led dried hibiscus sourcing

International buyers may request dried roselle calyces for tea blends, beverage manufacturing and ingredient distribution. We work with our Nigerian sourcing partner to assess suitable lots against each buyer's requirements.

Quality and sample requirements

Specify intended use, botanical identity, colour, moisture, cleanliness, foreign matter limits, packing, destination and any required laboratory testing, including pesticide residues and microbiological criteria. Each supply lot requires separate verification.

Small orders considered

We aim to start with representative samples and modest contracted orders, then scale through repeat transactions. Availability, export documentation, shipment terms and payment arrangements are confirmed before procurement.

Privacy notice

How we handle business enquiries and commercial correspondence.

Who we are

Staurus Foods Limited is a Nigerian business. For privacy or commercial enquiries write to info@staurusfoods.com.

What we receive

When you contact us, we may receive your name, company, business email, destination, product specifications and correspondence. The current enquiry form opens your email application; it does not automatically transmit or store a submission on this website.

How we use the information

We use enquiries to respond, provide commercial information, discuss samples and quotations, manage potential trade relationships, and meet legal obligations. The applicable legal basis depends on the circumstances and relevant data protection law.

Sharing and transfers

We may share relevant information with staff, sourcing partners, professional advisers and service providers where necessary to consider a transaction. International business correspondence may involve transfers between Nigeria and other countries. We apply safeguards where required by law. We do not sell buyer contact lists.

Retention and security

We retain correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to manage enquiries, commercial dealings, disputes and statutory duties. Access is restricted on a need-to-know basis. This Release 1 website does not use a customer database.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, object to processing and contact the relevant authority. Email info@staurusfoods.com with your request.

Cookies and future changes

This initial static website does not intentionally deploy marketing or analytics cookies. Essential security and access logs may be maintained by the hosting provider. This notice will be updated if analytics or a customer database is introduced.

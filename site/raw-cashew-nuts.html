Nigeria origin · Commodity supply enquiries

Nigerian raw cashew nuts for international processors

In-shell cashew nuts are sourced for overseas processors. Our sourcing partner will assess lot availability against the importing buyer’s specifications.

For international importers

Specification-led sourcing

Nut count, kernel outturn ratio (KOR), moisture, foreign material and defect levels can materially affect price and commercial acceptance.

Samples and documentation

Buyers should provide the required KOR, nut count per kilogram, acceptable moisture, packing method and intended destination. Samples and laboratory or cutting tests may be arranged subject to confirmation.

Vietnam and India are established raw cashew processing destinations. We do not claim stocked quantities or guaranteed grades before independent verification.

How our export enquiries work

Share the required grade, quantity, destination, commercial terms and documentation. We will assess sourcing availability with our Nigerian sourcing partner before issuing a transaction-specific quotation.

Initial small orders can be considered. Pricing, availability, inspection and payment arrangements must be agreed in writing for each transaction.
